Current role. I work primarily as a front-end developer on internal platform applications used by AlixPartners consultants.

The stack is Vue, React, TypeScript, and Pinia on the front end, with .NET on the back. Most of my contributions are front-end; I pick up back-end work when the feature calls for it.

I collaborate directly with product managers and designers to shape tickets and make technical decisions. I also lead the team's effort to learn how to use AI effectively in day-to-day development — rolling out AI-assisted workflows and specification-driven development (SDD), and helping the team build good habits around when and how to use these tools.
